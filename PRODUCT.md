# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
The implementation brief explicitly selects Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. npm is the initial package manager because there is no existing application or lockfile.

## Users and purpose
French-speaking entrepreneurs, ETIs, business managers and accounting professionals discover management solutions and contact GlobalCluster. Existing customers reach the separate operational platforms. Workshops and factories are the documented audience for Gestion PR.

## Capabilities and constraints
Three solutions: Paie et RH, États financiers, and Gestion PR (pièces de rechange). The public site never implements operational applications, authentication or subscriptions. Published source capabilities and destinations are the factual boundary. No fabricated prices, compliance guarantees, customers, trials or service levels. No production deployment or purchases.

## Brand commitments
Preserve the original logo, orange/coral identity, exact homepage headline and supporting statement. French public content. Quartz-inspired hero/features, restrained Kibo-style cursors, shared SmoothUI-inspired footer, accessible light/dark/system themes. The user authorizes original substitutions for inaccessible licensed components.

## Evidence on hand
docs/redesign-prompt.md is the implementation authority; docs/redesign-review.md contains the earlier audit. Rechecked source captures are stored in docs/sources. Contact and resource details come from the published site. No verified legal entity registration or privacy policy is available.

## Product principles
Separate discovery from application access. Give each product only its documented depth. Preserve existing resource paths. Favor accessible semantic content over decorative effects.

## Accessibility & inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard and touch access, reduced motion, 320px minimum overflow check and 200% zoom. Certification is not claimed.

## Working assumptions
The complete user brief replaces repeated setup interviews, as it explicitly requests autonomous decisions and questions only for delivery blockers. The publication host remains undecided; preview indexing is disabled by default.
