# GlobalCluster website

GlobalCluster presents business management solutions and provides access to their platforms and supporting resources.

## Language

**Solution**:
A marketed GlobalCluster offering: Paie et RH, États financiers, or Gestion PR.
_Avoid_: Platform when referring to a presentation page.

**Platform**:
An operational application accessed through an external destination published by GlobalCluster.
_Avoid_: Website, product presentation.

**Paie et RH**:
The solution for payroll and human resources management for companies and accounting professionals.

**États financiers**:
The solution for preparing, publishing and archiving financial statements and accounting reports.

**Gestion PR**:
The spare parts management offering for workshops and factories. PR means pièces de rechange in this website's context.
_Avoid_: Public relations, purchase requests.

**Resource**:
A published presentation, document or instructional video supporting solution discovery or use.

**Platform access**:
The visitor journey to an existing operational application, distinct from discovering a solution or contacting the company.
